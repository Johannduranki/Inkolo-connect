# SOP 05 - Member Dashboard

## Purpose

This SOP explains how a registered member uses the Duranki member dashboard.

## Access

Route:

`/dashboard/member`

Members sign in using one phone number and PIN/password.

## First Login

1. Open the login page.
2. Enter cellphone number.
3. Enter PIN/password.
4. Select Sign in.
5. Confirm the correct community is shown.

If the member belongs to more than one community or dashboard, use the switcher.

## Community Selection

If the member must choose a community:

1. Select Community Service.
2. Choose the correct community from the dropdown.
3. Confirm the selection.

If the community is wrong:

- Select Change Community.
- Sign in again and choose correctly.
- This can only be done once without admin approval.

## Community Membership Payment

If the member sees a Community Membership required message:

1. Select Pay Membership.
2. Read the membership information.
3. Accept the terms.
4. Choose Pay from Wallet or Pay by Card.
5. Confirm payment.
6. Return to the dashboard.

After successful payment, full community services should unlock.

## Activate A Service

1. Go to Duranki services.
2. Select the service card.
3. Review service details.
4. Select a plan if required.
5. Read and accept terms.
6. Select Confirm Subscription or Apply Now.
7. Check that the service appears under My Active Services.

## Open An Active Service

1. Go to My Active Services.
2. Find the service.
3. Select Open.

Common active services:

- Duranki Wallet
- My Community
- Community Membership
- Marketplace
- Job Search
- VAS Services
- Grant Distribution
- Trader Training
- Vuma Force
- Catch a Lift
- Funeral Services

## Duranki Wallet

Use the wallet to:

- View balance.
- Top up.
- Cash out.
- Transfer to another member.
- Pay for services where enabled.

For Community Membership:

1. Make sure wallet balance is enough.
2. Select Community Membership.
3. Select Pay from Wallet.

If balance is too low, top up first or pay by card.

## Trader Training

1. Open Trader Training from My Active Services.
2. View current module.
3. Complete the lesson.
4. Complete the practical exercise.
5. Complete the quiz.
6. Pass the module.
7. Funding milestone unlocks if configured.
8. Next module becomes available.

Future modules stay locked until the current module is passed.

## Grant Distribution

1. Open Grant Distribution.
2. View only your own grant record.
3. Check application status.
4. Check approved amount.
5. Check paid amount.
6. Check payment reference.
7. Check reconciliation status.
8. Follow the next step shown on the screen.

## Support

Contact your community administrator if:

- Your community is wrong.
- Your service does not appear.
- Your payment is not showing.
- Your ID or profile details are wrong.
- You cannot access your dashboard.
