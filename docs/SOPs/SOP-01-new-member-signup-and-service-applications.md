# SOP 01 - New Member Signup And Service Applications

## Purpose

This SOP explains how staff should guide a new person to register on Duranki, choose the correct community, enter their dashboard, and apply for services.

## Who Uses This SOP

- Duranki support staff
- Tenant staff
- Community administrators
- Field agents helping members register
- Call centre or onboarding staff

## Before You Start

Confirm the person has:

- A working cellphone number
- A PIN/password they can remember
- Their name and surname
- Their ID document if ID verification is required
- The correct community name

## Step 1 - Open The Platform

1. Open the Duranki login page.
2. Local testing URL: `http://localhost:4200/login`
3. Live production URL will be the official Duranki or tenant domain.

## Step 2 - Start Registration

1. Select the register/signup option.
2. Enter the member's cellphone number.
3. Enter the member's PIN/password.
4. Enter name and surname.
5. Upload or scan the ID document if required.
6. Check that the ID number, date of birth, gender, name, and surname are correct.

If the ID details are wrong, do not continue until the details are corrected.

## Step 3 - Existing Account Check

If the system finds an existing account:

1. Explain that Duranki uses one login across all communities and dashboards.
2. Show the member the communities already linked to their profile.
3. Ask if they want to register for another community.
4. Select the correct community from the dropdown.
5. Continue registration.

Important: A person should not create a second account just because they joined another community.

## Step 4 - Choose Community

1. Ask the member which community they belong to.
2. Select the correct community from the dropdown.
3. Confirm the community name with the member before continuing.
4. Complete the registration.

If the wrong community is selected:

- The member may use Change Community once.
- After one self-change, another change request must go to Duranki Admin.

## Step 5 - Privacy Policy

1. Open the Duranki Privacy Policy from the register page.
2. Give the member time to read it.
3. Explain that they must accept the policy to register.
4. Tick the acceptance checkbox only when the member agrees.

## Step 6 - First Login

After registration:

1. The member signs in using their cellphone number and PIN/password.
2. If they belong to a community, the community/member dashboard is their home page.
3. If they have more than one role, they use the dashboard switcher.

## Step 7 - Community Membership Fee

If the community has Community Membership enabled:

1. The member will see a Community Membership payment notice.
2. Explain that full platform access is limited until the fee is paid.
3. The member can pay using Duranki Wallet or bank card.
4. After payment, the member gets full access to the enabled community services.

## Step 8 - Apply For Services

1. On the member dashboard, go to Duranki services.
2. Select the service card.
3. Read the service information.
4. Select a plan if the service has plans.
5. Accept the terms and conditions.
6. Select Confirm Subscription or Apply Now.

Examples:

- Wallet: activate the Duranki Wallet.
- Community Membership: pay the monthly access fee.
- Trader Training: apply and continue training modules.
- Grant Distribution: apply for grant support.
- Vuma Force: register interest or use Vuma services.
- Funeral Services: choose the correct cover plan.
- Marketplace: activate buying and selling.

## Step 9 - Confirm Service Is Active

After applying or subscribing:

1. Check the My Active Services section.
2. Confirm the service appears there.
3. Open the service to check the correct dashboard or service screen.

## Common Problems

If the member cannot see a service:

- Check that the community has that service enabled.
- Check that the member is in the correct community.
- Check that Community Membership is paid if it is required.
- Ask Duranki Admin to check the tenant/community service configuration.

If the member lands on the wrong dashboard:

- Check their role switcher.
- Confirm their community registration.
- Sign out and sign in again if the role or community was changed.
