PRIORITY FAULTS

1. Community scope is lost when entering services.
   Reproduced: select Member - ZUC Community, then More Services.
   The destination is /dashboard/member?from=community, without communityId.
   The page then shows Traders KZN and its trader services.
   Community wallet/marketplace/jobs links also omit communityId in their hrefs.
   This is separate from the role switcher itself, which passed direct tests.
   Fix: preserve and validate community scope on every service entry and return
   path; scope subscriptions and service availability on the backend as well.

2. Service-administration destinations are not implemented in the served router.
   All 19 advertised /dashboard/services/<code> destinations are absent from
   the served Angular route table. Its wildcard destination is login.
   Explicit login redirects observed for Marketplace, Jobs, Airtime, Utilities,
   Grant Distribution, Trader Training, Internet and Insurance.
   Payments, Wallet, Community Membership and Vuma Force were briefly observed
   at their requested URLs; those transient results are NOT passes. Their routes
   are absent too. The remaining catalogue destinations were checked statically.
   A repeated-navigation run returned to login and required the user to sign in
   again; the cause of that session interruption was not established.
   Do not equate an advertised service or a successful HTML response with a
   working service-admin dashboard.

3. Accounting has no matching API implementation in the current backend.
   Live message: Accounting data could not be loaded from the server.
   The served client calls /api/platform/accounting and child endpoints for
   employees, payments, invoices, ledger entries, reconciliation and scope codes.
   No matching routes were found in the mounted platform API.
   New journal entry opens and cancels, but posting was NOT tested or certified.
   Live financial overview still displays sample assets/equity of R980,000 and
   a fixed monthly increase while other totals are zero. Periods are fixed to
   June-August 2026 and year-to-date, despite the October audit date.

4. Sample community information is still presented as real.
   Community wallet card shows 850.00 Izaka and a recent +250.00 payment, while
   the actual wallet screen shows 0.00 and no transactions.
   Traders KZN still displays a faith-community description, Sunday Mass,
   June 2026 service details, a sample pastor and sample contact details.
   The trader daily insight itself is correctly selected; ZUC shows scripture.
   Do not trust these sample cards as financial or community records.

5. Real payment connections are incomplete.
   Wallet Top Up, Cash Out and Transfer buttons open their forms.
   Top Up claims a Cycle checkout, but the backend explicitly returns 503 for
   card top-ups in real-data mode until a provider is connected.
   Cash-outs are similarly blocked pending a verified payout provider.
   Treasury explicitly states that bank transfers are not connected and that
   payment instructions must be processed through a bank separately.
   No money was moved and no provider settlement was tested.

6. Trader Training frontend/backend mismatch.
   The served client calls /api/platform/trader-training/me and assessment
   attempt endpoints. No corresponding mounted platform routes were found.
   Training completion, grant approval and milestone payouts are NOT certified.

7. Incomplete community utility controls.
   View directions, View calendar, Send a message (pastor card), and View
   community friends have no click bindings in the current source template.
   Browser attempts did not establish a working destination or dialog. An open
   native selector interfered with some late clicks, so the click-only result
   is inconclusive; the missing implementation remains a source finding.

8. Deployment and tenant registry gaps.
   The editable Angular source and served compiled bundle contain different
   feature sets. A source rebuild is not yet a safe replacement for the bundle.
   Startup reports SQL access denied and falls back to the persistent local
   store; health labels this persistent-demo. This is not SQL production proof.
   The tenant staff workspace initially lists ZUC, Trader Connect and Inkolo.
   Ingwe appears in the platform's browser-held tenant list but not yet in the
   server-held staff registry. Register its workspace through the explicit
   platform Users & Permissions action before assigning staff.
   SQL-backed tenant staff management deliberately fails closed pending migration.
